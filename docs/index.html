How Quobby works

Three simple steps to better studying

1

Create your study material

Build flashcard decks by typing, scanning textbooks with the document scanner, or letting AI generate vocabulary for you. Import existing decks from Anki, Quizlet, CSV, or JSON files. Organise everything into folders with custom colours and icons.

2

Study with proven methods

Review cards using spaced repetition, which schedules reviews at optimal intervals so you remember more in less time. Choose from flip cards, multiple-choice quizzes, or typing practice. Track your progress with detailed statistics and streak tracking.

3

Stay motivated and learn together

Earn XP and level up as you study. Compete on leaderboards, complete daily challenges, and open prize boxes. Challenge friends to real-time group study sessions with live leaderboards, or use Teacher Mode for classroom quizzes.
