PRODUCT UPDATE · NOVEMBER 3, 2026

What's New in Quobby: Daily Games, Alphabet Practice and More

Quobby's latest iOS and Android updates add three daily games, interactive number puzzles, alphabet practice decks, and practical improvements to studying.

Quobby brings flashcards, focus tools, and study habits together in one app. The latest updates add more ways to practise between study sessions, from daily puzzles to ready-made alphabet decks.

Three daily games for a study break

Letter Ledger, Number Trail, and Riddle Master are now available in Quobby. Daily game completions are shared between Quobby and Qurator, so your profile reflects a game finished in either place.

Letter Ledger gives you an interactive keyboard and a letter tracker as you solve each word. You can try words with repeated letters, check guesses beyond the built-in word list, and see clearer feedback when a guess is invalid. Saved rounds sync automatically. You can also mark guessed letters green, yellow, or red, then clear all of your notes at once without removing your guesses or their counts.

Number Trail lets you build an expression by tapping number and operator tiles. If you give up, Quobby shows the target and a working solution. Riddle Master adds a daily riddle to the set.

Build alphabet decks with example words

Alphabet Practice adds starter decks across 21 supported languages. Choose an alphabet language and a translation language to get started. New alphabet decks include an example word for every letter, and you can hear letter pronunciations and manage your decks from Flashcards Home.

Alphabet examples now skip low-confidence fallback translations, and each example stays paired with its letter while the translation appears on the reverse side. Pronunciation is more reliable on Android; on iOS, a card with uppercase and lowercase forms speaks the letter only once.

Small improvements across both apps

Flashcard imports now preserve Unicode characters. Alphabet practice no longer adds an extra reminder, and Letter Ledger better handles repeated-letter guesses while keeping the daily answer unique.

On iOS, reports are grouped by content so you can see how often an item was reported and why. App review prompts wait until you finish a session, and existing profiles are preserved when you sign in during onboarding.

On Android, eligible new subscribers now see the 7-day Premium trial selected when they make a purchase.

Update Quobby on your device to try the new games and alphabet tools. For the earlier Alphabet Practice walkthrough, see how to build a letter deck in Quobby.
