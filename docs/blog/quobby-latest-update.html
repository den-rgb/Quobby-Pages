PRODUCT UPDATE · NOVEMBER 10, 2026

What's New in Quobby v3.0.6: Prize Boxes and Smarter Daily Games

Quobby v3.0.6 adds prize-box rewards, clearer Letter Ledger results, helpful Riddle Master hints, and a more reliable path into shared Qurator tutorials.

Quobby brings flashcards, focus tools, and study habits together in one app. Version 3.0.6 makes the reward and daily-game experience more satisfying, while making it easier to return to the right Qurator tutorial from a shared link.

Prize boxes make every reward a moment

When you finish studying, playing, or completing a task, rewards now arrive in a prize box. Tap the box when you are ready to reveal what you earned, instead of having the result disappear automatically.

That same reveal experience is used for the Welcome Back bonus, and the update includes polish for prize-box and all-red Letter Ledger feedback animations.

See more after every Letter Ledger round

Letter Ledger now shows your win distribution after a successful round, alongside automatic letter feedback. Your latest solve also appears in history immediately, so your progress is easier to read at a glance.

For players who want a sterner daily word challenge, category hints are no longer shown. Every round now begins with a blank slate.

Get a nudge in Riddle Master

Riddle Master now reveals another letter for each incorrect answer. It keeps the puzzle moving without giving away the answer at once.

Open the intended Qurator tutorial

Shared Qurator links now open the tutorial they point to. Whether a friend sends a board-game walkthrough or a cooking guide, the link takes you directly back to the lesson you meant to continue.
