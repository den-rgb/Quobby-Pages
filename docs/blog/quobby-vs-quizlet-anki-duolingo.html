FEATURES & COMPARISON · OCTOBER 21, 2026

Quobby vs. Quizlet, Anki, and Duolingo: One App for More of Your Study Day

A deck of flashcards can help you practise. A whole study routine can help you keep going. Here’s how Quobby and Qurator connect review, focus, collaboration, and interactive learning—and how that compares with apps built around particular strengths.

There’s no single best learning app for every person or subject. Quizlet makes it easy to practise with digital study sets. Anki gives learners a highly configurable flashcard and review workflow. Duolingo is built around structured language lessons. Each has earned a place in many learners’ routines.

Quobby takes a broader approach. It brings study materials, active recall, focus support, habits, progress, and group practice into one mobile study companion. Qurator extends that idea with interactive tutorials that creators can build and learners can follow. If you want one flexible home for more of your study day, Quobby can be the better all-round fit.

Start with the study material you already have

Quobby lets you create flashcards with text, images, voice recordings, and example sentences, then review them with flip cards, multiple choice, or typing practice. Spaced repetition schedules reviews over time so you revisit material instead of relying on one long cram session.

Already have decks elsewhere? Quobby supports importing from Anki, Quizlet, CSV, and JSON, and you can browse and share community decks. Premium AI-powered vocabulary generation can turn a topic into practice material; auto-translation and text-to-speech support language practice. Premium document scanning can extract text and help turn it into flashcards, and Premium mind-map generation can show how ideas connect.

Keep the rest of your study routine close by

Remembering information is one part of studying. Quobby also includes handwritten notes, a focus timer with app-blocking options, a habit tracker, progress statistics, streaks, XP, achievements, and rewards. You can challenge friends in real-time study sessions or run classroom quizzes with Teacher Mode and live leaderboards.

Reading and studying tools can be personal, too. Quobby includes dyslexia-friendly fonts, bionic reading, reading tints, syllable hints, a reading ruler, and a calm study mode. The app interface is available in seven languages, and vocabulary practice supports more than 21 languages.

Qurator turns knowledge into interactive tutorials

Some things are easier to learn by following a process than by memorising a stack of cards. Qurator lets creators make step-by-step tutorials with branching paths, quizzes, and video. Learners can follow interactive guides across topics such as board games, cooking, DIY, software, music, sports, and science. Creators can publish tutorials for the community, including paid tutorials.

Together, Quobby and Qurator cover two useful modes: practise what you need to remember, and follow an interactive path through something you want to learn. Explore Qurator or see the tutorial library.

How the study apps compare

These products serve different goals. This feature overview is about how their tools fit a study routine, not a claim that one app produces better learning outcomes. Features and availability can change by platform, region, and plan.

AppWhere it shinesHow it fits your study day
Quobby + Qurator Flashcards and spaced repetition alongside scanning, notes, mind maps, focus tools, habits, accessibility aids, group study, and interactive tutorials. A strong all-round choice if you want more of your study workflow together, from creating materials through practice and focus.
Quizlet Study sets, flashcards, study modes, and AI-supported tools such as practice tests and study guides. A familiar way to practise shared or self-made sets. Quobby is a better fit when you also want focus, habits, note-making, accessibility tools, and live group study in the same companion.
Anki Customisable flashcards, spaced-repetition reviews, media-rich cards, and sync through AnkiWeb. A great choice if you want to tune a dedicated flashcard system. Quobby adds more built-in support around creating, organising, and doing the rest of your study routine.
Duolingo Structured language lessons with reading, listening, speaking, and writing practice, plus motivating progress features. A focused language-learning course experience. Quobby can complement it with custom vocabulary decks, spaced review, translation, and study tools for other subjects too.

Which one is right for you?

Choose Quizlet when you want a straightforward way to study sets and use its range of practice modes. Choose Anki when you want deep control over a flashcard workflow and review schedule. Choose Duolingo when you want a structured course for language learning.

Choose Quobby when your study day needs more than cards: custom review, material creation, focus support, habits, accessible reading tools, and studying with friends. Add Qurator when you want to follow or publish an interactive tutorial. For many learners, that breadth makes Quobby the better all-round study companion—and importing existing decks makes it easier to bring your current cards with you.
