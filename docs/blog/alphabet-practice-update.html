PRODUCT UPDATE · OCTOBER 22, 2026

Meet Alphabet Practice: Build a Letter Deck in Quobby

Start learning a new writing system with a ready-made deck. Choose a country and language, practise its letters, and hear a pronunciation right from the card.

Learning an unfamiliar alphabet can feel like a lot of symbols to keep straight. The latest Quobby update makes it easier to get started: create a deck from built-in letter cards, then use the same flashcard practice you already know.

Create an alphabet deck from a country and language

Open the alphabet deck option from Flashcards Home and choose a country. If more than one supported language is available for that country, choose the language you want to study. Quobby then creates a deck with starter cards for that language’s letters or writing system, so you can begin practising without adding every card by hand.

The cards are designed for recognition practice. When a script has uppercase and lowercase forms, the card can show both together. Flip it to see the letter’s pronunciation, then tap Pronounce to hear it spoken. The control speaks the letter once, even when both forms appear on the card.

Keep your alphabet decks organised

Alphabet decks appear with your flashcard decks on Flashcards Home. You can edit a deck or delete it when you no longer need it, just as you would manage your other decks. The country and language choices give the deck a clear starting point, while regular review helps you return to the letters you are learning.

On Android, new cards remember your style

We also updated individual card creation on Android. When you make another card, Quobby carries forward your recent font, colour scheme, and preset styling choices, so you spend less time setting up each card. Deck themes continue to be managed at the deck level.

Try the new update

Update Quobby on iOS or Android, open Flashcards Home, and create an alphabet deck to get started. For a wider look at Quobby’s study tools, read our comparison of Quobby, Quizlet, Anki, and Duolingo.
